Youth empowerment initiative

Youth After-School & Mentorship Program

Organization Overview

Name: Youth Empowerment Initiative

Founded: 2018 in Pretoria

Target audience: Learners aged 10 to 18 from local schools who need academic support, mentorship, and positive after-school engagement.

Brief History: The Youth Empowerment Initiative was founded in 2018 in Pretoria to give young people a safe, supportive space after school. The organisation focuses on mentorship, academic support, and skill-building activities that encourage confidence and personal growth.

Mission Statement: To empower youth through education, mentorship, and recreation, helping them discover their potential and build brighter futures.

Vision Statement: To nurture a generation of confident, skilled, and socially responsible young leaders who make a positive impact in their communities.

Website Goals

The website will act as the heart of the organisation’s communication — a place where learners, parents, and volunteers can connect easily.

Goals

  • Raise awareness about the Youth After-School & Mentorship Program.
  • Encourage community involvement and volunteer participation.
  • Make it simple to access program schedules, activities, and registration forms.
  • Support fundraising and partnerships through online donations.
  • Use testimonials, success stories, and event highlights to demonstrate the positive changes the program brings.

Key Performance Indicators

  • Growth in monthly website visits and engagement.
  • Number of volunteer and participant sign-ups.
  • Newsletter subscription and contact form submissions.
  • Frequency and total value of donations received.

Current Website Analysis

At present, the organisation relies mainly on social media for outreach. While this helps with visibility, it does not provide a structured space for detailed program information or registration.

Strengths: Active community engagement and strong social presence.

Weaknesses: Limited functionality for registration, poor content organisation, and no centralised information hub.

Improvement: Develop a dedicated website that makes communication, registration, and resource sharing easier and more professional.

Proposed Website Features and Functionality

  • Homepage: Welcoming overview of the program, mission statement, and clear call-to-action buttons (Join, Donate, Volunteer).
  • About Us: Organisation history, mission, vision, and team profiles.
  • Programs: Detailed descriptions of activities such as sports coaching, computer skills workshops, homework assistance, and mentorship circles.
  • Events: Calendar of upcoming workshops and community events.
  • Contact Details: Inquiry form, location map, and contact details.
  • Volunteer and Donate Sections: Online forms for sign-ups and secure donation processing.

Design and User Experience

The website will feel youthful, energetic, and community-driven. It will use a clean layout with bright visuals that reflect empowerment and collaboration.

Colour Scheme: A palette of blue, white, and yellow — blue for trust and stability, white for clarity, and yellow for optimism and energy.

Typography: Headings use Poppins Bold for a modern and friendly impression; body text uses Lato Regular for readability; Playfair Display Italic can be used sparingly for testimonials.

Hierarchy: Clear contrast between headings and body text for readability.

Layout and Design Approach

A grid-based layout with consistent spacing and intuitive navigation, with clear headings and visual cues to guide users.

User Experience Considerations

  • Simple navigation bar with dropdown menus.
  • Responsive design for mobile and desktop.
  • Accessible colour contrast and readable fonts.
  • Clear calls to action (Join, Volunteer, Donate).
