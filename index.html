Youth Empowerment Initiative provides a safe, supportive after-school environment for learners aged 10–18 through education, mentorship, recreation and practical skills.

Our purpose

A place to feel supported and inspired

Founded in Pretoria in 2018, the initiative connects learners, parents, mentors and volunteers around positive after-school engagement.

Academic support

Homework assistance and study support help learners strengthen their confidence and school performance.

Mentorship

Mentorship circles create positive relationships and encourage personal growth, responsibility and leadership.

Recreation & skills

Sports, computer skills and practical activities encourage teamwork, creativity and healthy development.
