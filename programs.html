Programs & Activities

Practical opportunities that support learning, confidence, health and leadership.

What we offer

Activities designed around young people's needs

Homework Assistance

Dedicated study time, homework guidance and learning support for school subjects.

  • After-school study sessions
  • Homework guidance
  • Peer learning

Computer Skills Workshops

Beginner-friendly digital activities that help learners develop useful technology skills.

  • Digital literacy
  • Online research skills
  • Responsible technology use

Sports Coaching

Structured sports activities that encourage fitness, teamwork and discipline.

  • Team sports
  • Fitness activities
  • Leadership through sport

Mentorship Circles

Small-group conversations where learners can develop confidence and discuss goals.

  • Goal setting
  • Positive role models
  • Peer support
