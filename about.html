About the Initiative

Discover our history, purpose and the people behind the Youth Empowerment Initiative.

Our history

Founded to create opportunity after school

The Youth Empowerment Initiative was founded in 2018 in Pretoria to give young people a safe, supportive space after school.

The organisation focuses on mentorship, academic help and skill-building activities that encourage confidence and personal growth.

What guides us

Mission, vision and audience

Mission

To empower youth through education, mentorship and recreation, helping them discover their potential and build brighter futures.

Vision

To nurture a generation of confident, skilled and socially responsible young leaders who make a positive impact in their communities.

Target audience

Learners aged 10–18 from local schools who need academic support, mentorship and positive after-school engagement.
